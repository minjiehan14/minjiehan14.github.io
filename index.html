Welcome!

I am a PhD candidate in Information Systems & Technology at Simon Business School, University of Rochester. I am fortunate to work with and learn from Professors Huaxia Rui, Jinyang Zheng, Mikhail Lysyakov, and Yang Gao.

My primary field of interest is economics of AI and Digital Platforms. My research applies causal inference to study how business interaction between platforms and AI developers affects digital ecosystem, and how AI applications shapes economic outcomes on platforms.

I completed my M.S. in Business Analytics from University of Rochester, and my B.S. in Statistics from Fudan University.
